Plan your trip

Safari packing list

Light neutral clothes (avoid white, black and blue), long sleeves and trousers, fleece for cool mornings, hat, comfortable walking shoes, sandals, swimsuit. Sunglasses, SPF 30+ sunscreen, DEET repellent, binoculars, camera with spare batteries, reusable water bottle, torch, daypack. Passport with visa, insurance details, vaccination certificate, cash (USD or TZS), document copies.

Visa and entry

Most nationalities need a visa. Apply online at eservices.immigration.go.tz/visa or on arrival at main entry points (check current rules). Bring a passport valid 6+ months, a return ticket and proof of funds. A yellow fever certificate is needed if you travel from or through an endemic country.

Health and safety

Recommended: yellow fever, hepatitis A and B, typhoid and tetanus vaccines. Ask your doctor about malaria prevention, wear long sleeves and use repellent. Drink bottled or filtered water. Travel insurance is a must. Keep valuables out of sight in crowded places.

Common questions

Currency? Tanzanian shilling; US dollars are widely accepted in tourist areas.
English? Yes, though Swahili is most widely spoken.
Phone and internet? Local SIM or roaming; coverage can be limited in remote areas.
Tipping? Customary for guides and hotel staff. Ask us for guidelines.
